How to play

Tap a block to push it in the direction of its arrow. It only flies out if the path is clear. Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom. Clear every block before time or taps run out.

FAQ

Lost progress after switching phones? Progress is stored on your device, so it can't be moved to another device yet.
Can't watch an ad? Check your internet connection and try again in a few seconds.
Turn off music, sound or vibration? Tap the gear button on the home screen.
